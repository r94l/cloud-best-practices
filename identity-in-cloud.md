In today’s hybrid world, the old “network perimeter” doesn’t protect us the way it used to. Users are signing in from different cities, devices, networks, and even continents but the one thing that stays constant is identity.

That’s why tools like Conditional Access, MFA, and least privilege in Microsoft Entra ID have become the real frontline of security. They don’t just block bad sign-ins, they understand context, adapt in real time, and make sure the right people have the right access at the right time.

I’ve seen situations where a stale Global Admin token left active after someone exited an organization caused major chaos. No firewall breach. No malware. Just identity. That’s the new perimeter we’re defending.

For anyone working in cloud or DevOps, strengthening identity isn’t just best practice anymore, it’s essential. Hybrid environments live or die by how well identity is managed.
